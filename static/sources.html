Compilation notes

  • This is a demo dataset, not a research database. Values come from different studies, seasons and trial conditions and are not directly comparable row-to-row.
  • Blank cells mean “not found in the consulted sources”, never zero.
  • Yield figures mix official potential yields, on-farm reports and single-trial means — the source and context travel with each number.
  • No affiliation with IRRI, BRRI, BINA or BRAC is claimed anywhere.
