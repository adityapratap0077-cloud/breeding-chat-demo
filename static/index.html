Rice Breeding — Demo Query Assistant

Ask plain-language questions over published trial data for rice varieties.

Trained only on rice. This assistant can't answer questions about anything except rice — wheat, other crops, and general topics are outside what it knows.
Demo, not a database. Answers come from 71 varieties compiled from published IRRI, BRRI, and BINA trials (sources listed here). Values come from different studies and seasons and are not strictly comparable. Built as a student demo — no affiliation with IRRI, BRRI, BINA, or BRAC.
Why a curated dataset instead of just asking an AI?

A general AI chatbot predicts plausible-sounding text. It can invent a yield figure, a release year, or a QTL with complete confidence — and you would never know. In breeding, a wrong number means wrong crosses and wasted seasons.

This demo does the opposite: it looks up values in published trial tables and shows the source behind every number. When the data isn't in the tables, it says so instead of guessing. That traceability is the whole point — an assistant a breeder can check, not just chat with.

Data: published trial tables (see sources). The assistant only answers from this dataset and says so when a question falls outside it.
